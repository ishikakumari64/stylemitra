# Style Mitra — Your AI-Powered Digital Wardrobe

> **Your Wardrobe. Your Style. Your AI Fashion Companion. ✨**

Style Mitra is an AI-powered wardrobe web application designed to make everyday outfit selection easier. Users can upload images of clothing items from their personal wardrobes and receive outfit suggestions tailored to different occasions.

From casual outings to parties and special events, Style Mitra explores how Artificial Intelligence can help users organize their wardrobes and discover outfit combinations using clothes they already own.

##  Key Features

- **Digital Wardrobe** — Upload and manage images of clothing items from your wardrobe.
- **AI-Powered Outfit Suggestions** — Get outfit recommendations based on your available clothing items.
- **Occasion-Based Styling** — Discover outfit ideas for different occasions and events.
- **Wardrobe-Based Recommendations** — Explore combinations using clothes you already own.
- **Personalized Style Discovery** — Make outfit selection easier with AI-assisted suggestions.

## How It Works

1. **Upload Your Clothes** — Add images of clothing items from your personal wardrobe.
2. **Build Your Digital Wardrobe** — Keep your clothing collection organized in one place.
3. **Choose an Occasion** — Select the type of event or outing you're dressing for.
4. **Get Outfit Suggestions** — Let the AI-powered recommendation workflow suggest suitable outfit combinations from your wardrobe.
